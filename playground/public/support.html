Support

Composer.nb helps you write, hear and edit music in saved notebooks. It is built and maintained by Pezanne Khambatta.

Start with a musical idea

Open Composer.nb in your assistant and tap a starter, or ask directly:

  • Write a Dorian vamp and melody that highlight the major sixth.
  • Build a major ii–V–I, then move the same chart through three keys.
  • Demonstrate modal interchange with playable examples from parallel major and minor.

You do not have to choose every setting. The assistant can pick a suitable key, tempo and instrumentation, then save playable music. Mention any settings you want it to preserve.

Connect and troubleshoot

  1. Sign in to Composer.nb with email or Google.
  2. Connect Composer.nb in your assistant and approve the notebook permissions shown.
  3. Ask for a piece, open its saved notebook and press Play. If audio is silent, check your device volume and click Play again; browsers require a user gesture for audio.
  4. If a save fails, keep the editor open, copy important source text and retry after checking your connection. A notebook should say it is saved before you leave.

Manage or revoke access in Connected apps. Revoking a connection does not delete your notebooks. The language reference explains modes, intervals, chords and rhythm.

Contact us

For account help, privacy requests, deletion requests or security reports, email khambatta.pezanne@gmail.com. For a general bug or feature request, use GitHub issues. GitHub issues are public: never include passwords, tokens, account details or private notebook contents.

For a bug, tell us the host you used, what you expected, what happened and a small non-private example if possible. Support is provided by the maintainer; there is no guaranteed response time.
