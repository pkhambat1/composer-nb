Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Composer.nb is operated by Pezanne Khambatta. This policy covers the Composer.nb website and its music-notebook connection for AI assistants. Contact khambatta.pezanne@gmail.com about privacy or your account.

What we process

  • Account details: your email and account identifier, and the name and profile image provided when you sign in with Google. Authentication is handled by Supabase.
  • Notebook contents: titles, music source, text cells, cell identifiers, revisions and timestamps. These are stored in your account so you can reopen and edit them.
  • Connected-app records: the client you authorize, permissions, expiry and revocation information, and hashed OAuth credentials. Your browser stores session information and display preferences.
  • Service requests: hosting and authentication providers may process IP addresses, device and browser information, request URLs and operational logs to deliver and protect the service.
  • Support messages: information you choose to email us.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to sign you in, save and render music, connect the assistant you authorize, resolve support requests and protect the service. Vercel hosts the website and server; Supabase provides authentication and database storage. Audio playback downloads instrument samples from GitHub Pages and jsDelivr, which receive ordinary network-request information.

When you connect an assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude, that host can request notebook information and make edits within the permissions you grant. Requested notebook contents and tool results are returned to that host. Its own privacy policy governs how it handles your conversations and results. Composer.nb does not include an advertising or model-training pipeline.

Export and sharing links

Sheet-music and MIDI links can contain the music source, including preceding cells, in their URL. Anyone with such a link can retrieve that content without signing in. These responses may be cached for up to one year, and recipients can keep copies. Deleting a notebook does not revoke an exported link or copies already shared. Avoid including private notes in material you export or share.

Retention and your choices

Saved notebooks remain in the database until they are permanently removed. The notebook Delete action currently hides a notebook from the library; it does not immediately erase its database record. To request account deletion, permanent deletion of stored notebooks, access or correction, email us from the address associated with your account. We may need to verify ownership before acting. Operational records and provider backups may remain according to the providers’ retention processes or legal requirements; we do not promise an immediate purge of every backup.

You can edit your notebooks, export music, sign out, or revoke an assistant’s access in Connected apps. Revocation stops future access through that connection; it does not remove information already received by the host. Browser settings let you clear locally stored session information and preferences.

Security, children and changes

Account ownership checks and scoped connections restrict access, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not put passwords or other secrets in notebooks. Composer.nb is not intended for children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information. We will update this page when our practices change, with the revision date above.
