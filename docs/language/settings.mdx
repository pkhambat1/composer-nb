A setting is a name followed by its value, with no `=` or `:`. It applies from its line to the end of the cell, or of the block it is in.

| Setting | Value | Default | Sets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `time` | `<count> over <unit>` | `4 over 4` | The time signature. |
| `tempo` | 20 to 400 | `120` | Quarter notes per minute. |
| `swing` | 50 to 75 | `50` | Where the off-beat eighth falls, as a percentage of the beat. |
| `kit` | `rock` or `synth` | `rock` | The drum kit. |
| `key` | `C`, `Am`, `Bb`, `F#m` and so on | `C` | The key [Roman numerals](/language/chords#roman-numerals) are read in. |
| `capo` | 0 to 12 | `0` | The fret a guitar's chords are played from. |
| `octave` | 1 to 6 | `3` | The octave of a note without a number, and of a chord's root. |
| `guitar.electric` | `clean` or `muted` | `clean` | The electric guitar's sound. |

## time

The unit is the note value counted: 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16. The count can be a sum, as in `time 3+4 over 8`, which groups the grid without changing the bar. `over` is required because `/` is division: `7/8` is the number 0.875.

## swing

`swing 67` plays the off-beat eighths at two thirds of the beat, a triplet feel; `50` is straight and `75` dotted. Sixteenths move with the eighth they belong to. Only the sound moves: the grid and the sheet stay as written.

<Cell>

```composer-nb
swing 67
tempo 100

play {
  ride: x-x- x-x- x-x- x-x-
  hat.pedal: 2 4
  kick: 1 3
}
```

</Cell>

## kit

`rock` is a recorded kit, loaded from the network on first use. `synth` is synthesized and works offline.

## capo and octave

`capo` raises a guitar's chords by that many frets. Their names and diagrams keep the shape you finger. It does not move notes, since a note's name is its pitch.

`octave` counts from C: `C4` is middle C, and the note below it is `B3`.

## Instrument sound

An instrument's name followed by a value, with no colon, sets its sound from that line on. `guitar.electric muted` is palm-muted. With a colon, the same name starts a line. Only `guitar.electric` has more than one sound.
