A setting is `set`, then the setting and its value: `set tempo 90`. It applies from its line to the end of the cell, or of the block it is in. A block's lines play together, so its `time`, `tempo` and `swing` are set above all of them; `key`, `octave`, `capo`, `kit` and a sound can change between its lines.

| Setting | Value | Default | Sets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `time` | `<count> over <unit>` | `4 over 4` | The time signature. |
| `tempo` | 20 to 400 | `120` | Quarter notes per minute. |
| `swing` | 50 to 75 | `50` | Where the off-beat eighth falls, as a percentage of the beat. |
| `kit` | `rock` or `synth` | `rock` | The drum kit. |
| `key` | `C`, `Am`, `Bb`, `F#m`, or a root and a mode: `D dorian` | `C` | The key: the root that [Roman numerals](/language/chords#roman-numerals) and [degrees](/language/notes#degrees) count from, and the sheet's key signature. |
| `capo` | 0 to 12 | `0` | The fret a guitar's chords are played from. |
| `octave` | 1 to 6 | `3` | The octave of a note without a number, of degree 1, and of a chord's root. |
| `guitar.electric` | `clean` or `muted` | `clean` | The electric guitar's sound. |

## time

The unit is the note value counted: 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16. The count can be a sum, as in `set time 3+4 over 8`, which groups the grid without changing the bar. `over` is required because `/` is division: `7/8` is the number 0.875.

## swing

`set swing 67` plays the off-beat eighths at two thirds of the beat, a triplet feel; `50` is straight and `75` dotted. Sixteenths move with the eighth they belong to. Only the sound moves: the grid and the sheet stay as written.

<Cell>

```composer-nb
set swing 67
set tempo 100

play {
  ride: 1 bars x-
  hat.pedal: 2 4
  kick: 1 3
}
```

</Cell>

## key

A major key is its letter, a minor key its letter and `m`, and a mode its root and the mode's name: `D dorian`, `E phrygian`, `F lydian`, `G mixolydian`, `B locrian`, with `ionian` and `aeolian` as the modal names of major and minor.

Roman numerals and degrees count on the major scale of the key's root, whatever the key's mode, and `b` or `#` in front moves them a semitone. That is how a scale is described against major: natural minor is `1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7`, and its chords are `i bIII iv v bVI bVII`. The minor or the mode sets the sheet's key signature: `D dorian` has none, `A mixolydian` two sharps.

<Cell>

```composer-nb
set key D dorian
set tempo 96

pattern vamp = {
  piano: chords i IV|i bVII
  bass: 2 beats D2, 2 beats G2 | 2 beats D2, 2 beats C2
  hat: x-
  kick: 1 3
  snare: 2 4
}

play vamp
```

</Cell>

The major `IV` under a minor `i` is what makes dorian dorian: `i IV` is Dm G, and `bVII` is C.

## kit

`rock` is a recorded kit, loaded from the network on first use. `synth` is synthesized and works offline.

## capo and octave

`capo` raises a guitar's chords by that many frets. Their names and diagrams keep the shape you finger. It does not move notes, since a note's name is its pitch.

`octave` counts from C: `C4` is middle C, and the note below it is `B3`.

## Instrument sound

`set` with an instrument's name and a sound sets that instrument's sound from its line on: `set guitar.electric muted` is palm-muted. Only `guitar.electric` has more than one sound.
